Setup steps from Apple

Turn on account-driven enrolment (User or Device)
  1. Sign in to Apple Business with a role that can manage devices.
  2. Go to Devices > Management Services, then the Device Enrollment tab.
  3. Choose "Enrol as personal device" for User Enrolment, or "Enrol as organization-owned device" for Device Enrolment, for each device type.
Set up Automated Device Enrolment (user or group Blueprint)
  1. Link your Apple Customer Number or Reseller Number under device suppliers.
  2. Assign each device to the Apple Business device management service. If it does not appear, add it with Apple Configurator.
  3. Keep devices powered on and online. The user completes Setup Assistant on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple TV finishes on its own.
  4. The user signs in with their Managed Apple Account.
Approve devices enrolled with a device Blueprint
  1. A role that can purchase subscriptions must approve each device. Tick "Approve recently added devices for management without manual review" when confirming the Blueprint to do this up front, for devices never approved before.
  2. Otherwise go to Devices > Inventory and select the device. You can paste up to 1,024 comma-separated serial numbers to search.
  3. Check the enrolment date, OS and certificate fingerprint (SHA-256). On Mac it is in Keychain > Certificates > Systems. On iOS it is in Settings > your account > More Details > Device Identity Certificate.
  4. Approve if it matches. Deny if not, which removes the enrolment profile.
Send enrolment instructions to users
  1. Sign in with a role that can create, edit and delete Managed Apple Accounts.
  2. Go to People > Users. For one user, select them and choose Enrol Devices. For several, select them and choose Send Device Enrolment Instructions.
  3. Choose Mac or iPhone / iPad / Apple Vision Pro instructions and select Send. Users follow the link in the email.

After enrolment the Apple Business app installs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro (not Apple TV). Users get assigned work apps, a list of their managed devices and AppleCare+ for Business support. Devices seven years past introduction may work but are not supported for Automated Device Enrolment.
